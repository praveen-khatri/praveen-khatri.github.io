A fascination with

My story

Turning biochemical
unknowns into answers.

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Biology at the University of Toronto Mississauga, working with Michael A. Phillips on specialized diterpenoid metabolism in Salvia divinorum.

My earlier work with Sangeeta Dhaubhadel at Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada and Western University focused on cytochrome P450 monooxygenases, soybean isoflavonoid pathways and resistance to Phytophthora sojae. Those projects shaped my interest in how biosynthetic enzymes evolve and how their catalytic capabilities reshape plant chemical defense.

Before moving to Canada, I trained in molecular biology and biotechnology in India and worked on the function and evolution of plant aquaporins and stress-associated transport processes.

Across these experiences, I have increasingly focused on pairing experimental biochemical discovery with comparative and systems-level analysis.
