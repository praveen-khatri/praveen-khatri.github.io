01 / Current research · Ongoing research

Reconstructing salvinorin biosynthesis

Connecting candidate genes with the late-stage enzymatic steps that diversify Salvia divinorum diterpenoids.

At the University of Toronto Mississauga, I integrate elicitor-responsive transcriptomics, metabolite profiling, comparative sequence analysis and heterologous functional assays to investigate tailoring reactions in the salvinorin A pathway. The work combines candidate prioritization with experimental validation rather than relying on sequence similarity alone.

Research question

Which enzymes carry out the unresolved transformations leading to structurally complex diterpenoids?

02 / Published discovery · Published · Molecular Plant

The final steps of glyceollin biosynthesis

Identifying the cytochrome P450 enzymes that complete a central soybean defense pathway.

My work with Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada and Western University helped discover and functionally characterize three cytochrome P450 prenyl cyclases involved in the terminal reaction of glyceollin biosynthesis. This research connects specialized-metabolite chemistry to plant defense and is published in Molecular Plant (2025).

Research question

How does soybean convert prenylated isoflavonoids into antimicrobial glyceollins?

03 / Preprint · Preprint · bioRxiv

Catalytic diversity of isoflavone hydroxylases

Exploring how related CYP81E enzymes produce chemically distinct isoflavonoids.

A systematic functional study of soybean isoflavone hydroxylases investigated substrate and regioselectivity and identified additional hydroxyisoflavone products. Combined biochemical and evolutionary analysis informs how gene-family diversification expands biosynthetic possibilities. A 2026 bioRxiv preprint is available; the study has not yet completed peer review.

Research question

How do changes within a P450 family redirect metabolic flux toward new defense compounds?

04 / Systems biology · Ongoing analysis

Time-resolved plant–pathogen responses

Understanding the transcriptional architecture of partial resistance to Phytophthora sojae.

I study soybean defense through time-resolved transcriptome analysis, comparing infected and control roots across susceptible and partially resistant genetic backgrounds. Differential expression, co-expression networks and pathway-level analysis support the discovery of pathways and regulatory hypotheses that can be tested experimentally.

Research question

When and how do resistant and susceptible soybean roots diverge after infection?
